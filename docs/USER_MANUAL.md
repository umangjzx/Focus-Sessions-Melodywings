# Focus Sessions — User Manual

## Getting started

1. Visit the app and click **Get started** on the landing page.
2. Create an account with your name, email, and password.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard** with today's stats and a **Quick Start** button.

## Focus sessions

1. Go to **Focus** in the sidebar (or Quick Start).
2. Enter a session title, optional task, duration, Pomodoro preset, and ambient sound.
3. Tap **Begin Focus Session** — watch the 3-2-1 countdown.
4. Use **Pause**, **Fullscreen**, or **End** as needed.
5. When the timer ends, enjoy a **break** or complete the session.

## After a session

Rate your mood (1–5), mark the task done if applicable, and add reflection notes. You'll see your productivity score, XP earned, and any new badges.

## Tasks

Create tasks with priority levels and optional subtasks. Check them off when done — completed tasks boost your session score.

## Ambient sounds

Choose from white noise, brown noise, rain, forest, ocean, or lo-fi during setup. Control playback and volume in focus mode.

## Analytics & achievements

- **Stats** — weekly minutes, productive hours, completion rate
- **Rewards** — badges for milestones like 7-day streaks and 25 hours focused

## Settings

Adjust default session length, light/dark theme, notifications, auto-breaks, volume, and the AI focus coach.

## Tips for ADHD users

- Start with 15–25 minute sessions
- Link each session to one clear task
- Use the countdown — it signals your brain to switch modes
- Celebrate small wins with XP and streaks
